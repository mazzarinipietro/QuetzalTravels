CULTURE

A Culture Woven in Color and History

Guatemala is a land of enduring heritage, where the legacy of the Maya lives on in vibrant traditions, languages, and customs passed through generations.

From intricately woven textiles to ancestral ceremonies and close-knit communities, Guatemalan culture is a rich tapestry of identity, resilience, and pride.

Maya Heritage

Ancient temples and carvings tell stories of a civilization that continues to inspire.

Weaving Traditions

Bright handwoven textiles crafted with ancestral backstrap techniques carry deep symbolic meaning in every thread.

Community Spirit

Vibrant local markets and close-knit village life reveal the resilience, warmth, and pride of the Guatemalan people.

FESTIVALS

Celebrations That Echo Through Time

In Guatemala, every festival is a reflection of devotion, remembrance, and community.

Rooted in centuries-old traditions, these celebrations unite people in faith and joy, carrying forward stories and customs that have shaped the soul of the nation.

Semana Santa

Solemn processions, incense, and intricate alfombras transform towns in a profound expression of faith.

Día de los Muertos

A heartfelt tribute where families adorn graves with flowers, candles, and offerings to honor their beloved ancestors.

Feria de Jocotenango

A vibrant traditional fair filled with music, rides, food, and ancestral customs.

CUISINE

Flavors Rooted in Ancient Tradition

Guatemalan cuisine is a delicious blend of Maya heritage and Spanish influence, passed down through generations.

With corn as its heart and local ingredients as its soul, every dish tells a story of land, family, and tradition.

Pepián

Guatemala's oldest recipe — a thick stew of roasted seeds, chili, and spices with deep pre-Hispanic roots.

Kak'ik

A ceremonial turkey soup from the Q'eqchi' Maya, with a vivid red broth and bold aromatic spices.

Tamales Colorados

Tender tamales filled with spiced meat and red sauce, wrapped in banana leaves — a festive Guatemalan staple.

Tostadas

Crispy corn tostadas layered with refried beans, marinated meats, pickled vegetables, and fresh garnishes.

Atol de Elote

A warm, sweet corn drink with a thick, velvety texture, cherished across generations as a comforting home tradition.

Rellenitos

Ripe plantains mashed, stuffed with sweetened black beans, and fried to a golden crisp — a beloved Guatemalan dessert.

NATURE

Wildlife Hidden in Ancient Forests

Guatemala is a biodiversity treasure: from misty highland cloud forests and lush jungles to Pacific and Caribbean coasts teeming with life.

National parks, nature reserves, and community-led conservation efforts protect these habitats and the extraordinary species that call them home.

Quetzal

Cloud Forests

Sacred to the Maya, the quetzal's shimmering emerald feathers embody freedom, beauty, and Guatemalan national identity.

Howler Monkey

Jungle Canopy

Its thunderous calls carry miles through the canopy — the howler monkey is the jungle's most emblematic voice.

Iguana

Volcanic Lowlands

Ancient and sun-loving, iguanas bask along Guatemala's volcanic lowlands and riverbanks in prehistoric stillness.

Mapache

Highlands & Rainforest

Clever and adaptable, mapaches roam Guatemala's forests and riversides with a curiosity that belies their shy nature.
