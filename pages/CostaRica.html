CULTURE

A Culture Rooted in Nature and Warmth

Costa Rican culture is a mosaic of indigenous traditions, Spanish influences, and community spirit. Hospitality here is an authentic value — felt in every encounter, every smile, and every daily gesture.

Colorful festivities, lively music, and ancient crafts tell a story of deep roots and pride for the land. In Costa Rica, culture is not something to observe — it is something to live.

Indigenous Art

Ancient symbols and masterful artisanship passed down through generations of skilled Boruca and Chorotega hands.

Pura Vida

More than a greeting — a philosophy of life that embraces joy, gratitude, and the beauty of simplicity.

Connection with Nature

A deep bond with the land that inspires daily respect, sustainable living, and harmony with the wild.

FESTIVALS

Celebrations That Tell a Story

In Costa Rica, every celebration is a story rooted in history, faith, and community.

From traditional dances to religious ceremonies, every event is a chance to honor the past and strengthen the bonds that unite people.

Fiesta de los Diablitos

A Boruca ritual where masked dancers reenact the ancestral struggle against the Spanish in a burst of color and spirit.

Guanacaste Annexation Day

On July 25, 1824, Guanacaste voted to join Costa Rica — celebrated with parades, music, and national pride.

Independence Day

September 15 marks independence from Spain, celebrated with torch marches, school parades, and the Flame of Liberty relay.

CUISINE

Flavors Born from Volcanic Soil

Costa Rican cuisine is born from volcanic soil, authentic flavors, and traditions passed down through generations.

Fresh ingredients, simple recipes, and a great deal of care give life to dishes that tell the soul of the country and the warmth of its people.

Gallo Pinto

Sautéed rice and black beans seasoned with Lizano sauce — the iconic, beloved heart of the Costa Rican breakfast.

Casado

A complete plate of rice, beans, salad, plantain, and meat — the cornerstone of Costa Rican home cooking.

Ceviche

Fresh sea bass marinated in citrus juice with onion, cilantro, and a hint of spice — light and vibrant.

Tamales

Spiced corn dough stuffed with meat and vegetables, wrapped in banana leaves and steamed to perfection.

Costa Rican Coffee

Grown on rich volcanic slopes, Costa Rican arabica yields a smooth, aromatic cup celebrated worldwide.

Tres Leches

A sponge cake soaked in three types of milk — an irresistibly creamy centerpiece of festive Costa Rican tables.

NATURE

Wildlife That Will Change Your Perspective

Costa Rica hosts extraordinary biodiversity: over 5% of the world's species live here, despite the country covering less than 0.1% of the Earth's surface.

From rainforests to ocean floors, every ecosystem is a natural sanctuary protecting unique and fascinating creatures.

Sloth

Rainforests

A symbol of slowness and serenity, the sloth lives among the treetops reminding us to appreciate every moment.

Toucan

Forests and Jungles

With its colorful beak, the toucan lights up the tropical forest and plays a key role in seed dispersal.

Scarlet macaw

Tropical Rainforests

One of the most vibrant birds on Earth, the Scarlet Macaw soars through the dense rainforests of Central America with its dazzling red, yellow, and blue plumage.

Sea Turtle

Oceans and Beaches

Every year, sea turtles return to the beaches to lay their eggs, continuing a thousand-year life cycle.
